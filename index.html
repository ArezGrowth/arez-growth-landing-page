The product direction

Built for teams making decisions from sales data

Arez Growth is in development as an AI-powered sales intelligence SaaS and web application for B2B companies with sales teams and sales data.

01

Understand sales performance

The product is being designed to help users interpret what is happening in sales through analysis, KPI tracking, and performance visibility.

02

Focus attention where it matters

Arez Growth is being built to help identify sales problems and opportunities, so teams can evaluate which areas deserve attention based on their data.

03

Designed for practical oversight

The planned product direction gives sales managers and management a clearer way to review sales information and support decisions.

From data to insight

A planned workflow for clearer sales visibility

The planned Arez Growth workflow is centered on turning sales data into visibility, interpretation, and recommended areas of focus.

01

Bring sales data into the workflow

Arez Growth is being designed to support an Excel/CSV data workflow.

02

Calculate and monitor KPIs

The product is being built to calculate relevant sales KPIs and present them for performance review.

03

Surface insights and priorities

AI-powered insights and actionable recommendations are planned to help interpret sales data.
